# Risk Assessment Methodology

## Likelihood

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Rare |
| 2 | Unlikely |
| 3 | Possible |
| 4 | Likely |
| 5 | Almost certain |

## Impact

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 1 | Very low |
| 2 | Low |
| 3 | Moderate |
| 4 | High |
| 5 | Severe |

## Risk score

Risk score = Likelihood × Impact

- 1–4: Low
- 5–9: Medium
- 10–16: High
- 17–25: Critical
